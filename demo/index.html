ONE MESSAGE. ONE SAFE DECISION.

Who gets to speak
to people in the car?

SIA is a safety-aware translator between vehicle systems and their screens or voice channels. It does not decide whether an obstacle is real. It checks whether a warning is trusted, fresh, and delivered in the right place.
