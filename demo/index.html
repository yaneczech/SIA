        SAME CONTRACT, DIFFERENT POLICY
        Critical is not the default
        
        
        SIA is not only a security gateway for warnings. The declaration makes different lifecycle choices explicit for different interaction types.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        
          
        ALERT
        
          
        Collision warning
        
          
        • Restricted driver-assistance authority
        • Short semantic validity
        • Never held behind routine content
        • Declared next-eligible delivery fallback
        • Explicit response policy
        
        
        
        
        
          
        NOTIFICATION
        
          
        Now playing
        
          
        • Vehicle-service or third-party authority
        • Longer semantic validity
        • Coalesces to the latest value
        • Broader renderer eligibility
        • No acknowledgement required
        
        
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        
        
        BOUNDARIES
        What SIA is—and is not
        
        
        SIA has a defined boundary. Production safety still depends on the surrounding vehicle system and empirical evidence.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        SIA does
        • Preserve declared meaning and authority
        • Choose eligible outputs under explicit policy
        • Bind evidence of delivery and response
        
        
        SIA does not
        • Detect hazards or validate sensor truth
        • Draw the UI or replace renderer safety controls
        • Replace middleware, cybersecurity, functional safety, or HMI testing
        
        
        SIA depends on
        • Authenticated context and actor credentials
        • Empirically calibrated attention estimates
        • Deployment-owned safety and availability mechanisms
