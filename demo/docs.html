SEMANTIC INTERACTION ARCHITECTURE · 0.4

Who may say what to the people in a car—and how do you prove it?

A modern car has dozens of software systems, several screens, a voice assistant—and soon third-party apps and AI agents. All of them want your attention while you drive. SIA is a proposed checkpoint layer that decides which messages get through, where they appear, and keeps evidence of everything that happened.

WHAT BREAKS TODAY

Any system can look like a safety system.

Nothing in today’s vehicle stack structurally stops a music app or an AI assistant from drawing something that looks exactly like a collision warning—or from being drowned out by one that matters less.

WHY IT HAPPENS

Every screen keeps its own rules.

Each display and voice channel separately decides what to show and how urgent it looks. The rules live in every screen’s code, so they differ per screen, per car, per software update—and nobody can audit them in one place.

WHAT SIA CHANGES

One checkpoint, rules written once.

SIA moves the rules out of the screens into one shared checkpoint. Every message declares its meaning; the checkpoint verifies who sent it, checks it is fresh and relevant, picks the safest output, and demands proof of what happened.

One story runs through this whole page. Your car’s driver-assistance system (ADAS) detects a collision risk at highway speed. Follow that single warning from the day its rules were written to the moment the driver acknowledges it.

MEANINGDeclared once
POLICYEvaluated centrally
EVIDENCEBound end to end
RESULTPortable interaction integrity
SIA does not detect the hazard.

ADAS decides whether a collision risk is real. SIA decides whether the resulting interaction claim is authorised, fresh, context-appropriate, and eligible for presentation.

        03 · TIME & CONTEXT

        04 · DELIVERY & RESPONSE

        Two feedback loops, two different claims

        The plan is made—now prove what actually happened. A renderer can prove it presented something. Only a separate occupant response can prove a human acknowledged it. SIA never lets one claim stand in for the other.
